## 4. Science and standards evidence
- **(a) TDOA vs gNB sync:** **±50 ns gNB sync gives ~±15 m ranging error**, from a 3GPP-compliant 5G testbed: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18323 (Oct 2024) **[P, preprint]**. The O-RAN sync spec of about 65 ns is "too coarse for sub-meter" accuracy without calibration. Rel-17 targets are **1 m commercial and 20 cm IIoT**: https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/12618933 **[patent, medium]**. TR 38.857 Annex B.1 models gNB timing error; we did not extract its values: https://3gpp.org **[P]**. Physics: **1 ns ≈ 30 cm**, so cm-level positioning needs **≤100 ps** sync **[derived]**.
- **(b) Relay attacks:**
  - UK: **93%** of vehicles recovered by Tracker in Jan–Oct 2025 were stolen without keys (a 15-year high): https://www.tracker.co.uk/news-and-resources/legislation-must-be-enforced-keyless-thefts-soar **[Pr/industry]**
  - Admiral: **up to 70%** of thefts involved keyless cars (year to Nov 2025): https://www.deeside.com/keyless-car-thefts-hit-15-year-high-across-uk/ **[Pr]**
  - Netherlands: keyless entry on **59%** of stolen cars (2025), from Stichting VbV: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/06/05/keyless-car-theft-protection/ **[Pr]**
  - Auto Express: only **3 of 237** models tested resisted relay: https://www.autoexpress.co.uk/car-news/105809/almost-all-keyless-car-systems-vulnerable-to-relay-attacks **[Pr]**
  - 802.15.4z: a relay can only *increase* time of flight. Ghost Peak shows distance reduction from **12 m to 0 m** on commercial chips, and most Digital Key devices use a single STS segment: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05313 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03964 **[P, peer-reviewed/preprint]**
  - Physics: **1 ns of round-trip time ≈ 15 cm** **[derived]**
- **(c) Trusted time:**
  - **Roughtime is now RFC 10049** (Experimental, Oct 2026): https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc10049.html **[P]**
  - IETF drafts NTS-for-PTP and NTPv5 are active (2026): https://bgpupdates.potaroo.net/ietf/html/ids-wg-ntp.html **[P mirror]**
  - SEALSQ: PQC TPM with **ML-DSA-87 / ML-KEM-1024**. FIPS 140-3 submission targeted Sep 2026 and TCG certification Oct 2026, ahead of the 2027 US-government deadline: https://www.sealsq.com/investors/news-releases/sealsq-accelerates-post-quantum-readiness-with-next-generation-tpms-ahead-of-2027-u.s.-government-deadline **[P]**
  - **No specific SEALSQ trusted-time product found.** This is a gap and a possible partnership angle.
