# Letter-of-interest and outreach templates

## A. Digital letter of interest (non-binding; an email reply saying "confirmed" is enough)
Subject: Letter of interest, TVA Labs coherence module (non-binding)

[Organisation] has discussed with the TVA Labs team (NextSEMI Hackathon, SEMICON Europa 2026) their concept of a wire-free coherence module built around a chip-scale atomic clock with a short-term stability of 1e-15 at 1 s.

We confirm that, in our work on [use case, e.g. separate-module / multi-site apertures at 70–300 GHz], phase drift between independent references limits [what it limits, e.g. the usable aperture time to about X seconds / forces a cable or fibre between modules].

Subject to the device meeting its specification, we would be interested in evaluating a module in 2027 [optional: and in being named as a reference / design partner].

This letter expresses interest only and creates no obligation on either side.

Name, role, organisation, date.
[ ] You may name our organisation in the pitch.   [ ] Please keep us anonymous.

## B. Cold email (one question, one favour; 120 words)
Subject: One question on phase coherence in your [300 GHz / sub-THz] measurements

Dear [Dr/Prof] [name],

I am part of a four-person team at the NextSEMI venture hackathon at SEMICON Europa in Munich (deadline today 12:00 CET). We are testing whether a chip-scale atomic clock with 1e-15 stability at 1 s would let separate transmitters and receivers stay phase-coherent for about a minute at 300 GHz without a cable or fibre.

Your [paper title, year] is the closest published evidence we have. One question: in your campaigns, what limits the untethered measurement time today, and what would a minute of free-running coherence at 300 GHz change?

If you can reply with one or two sentences before 11:30 CET, we would quote you (anonymised unless you say otherwise). Happy to share our model and results in return.

Kind regards, [name], TVA Labs team

## C. LinkedIn message (50 words)
Hi [name], I'm on a team at the NextSEMI hackathon (SEMICON Europa, Munich) pitching wire-free phase coherence for multi-site radar, built on a 1e-15 chip-scale atomic clock. One question: when your sync link is denied, how long can a multistatic system coast today? A one-line answer before 12:45 CET would help us enormously.
