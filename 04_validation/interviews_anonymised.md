# Industry interviews: anonymised record and how the concept changed
Conducted 10–11 Nov 2026 on the SEMICON Europa / electronica floor by the team. Organisations are described by role; only the Fraunhofer centre gave permission to be named. Personal names are not recorded here. Stakeholder types covered: component supplier (3), research end user (1), system integrator (2), GNSS vendor (1), security vendor (1), T&M OEM (1), photonics startup (1).
Method: discovery first (problem, current solution, failure modes, buyer), technology last; see the interview guide. Evidence quality: V = verbatim quote, N = note, H = hearsay or ambiguous.

## How the concept changed, in order
1. Start (10 Nov): "a better clock for holdover and GNSS-denied PNT". Killed by 1 and 2: the market pays for aging, which the challenge freezes.
2. Drone anti-spoofing TDOA (11 Nov, team deck). Demoted after our own critique: once realistic link noise is included the gain is 1.3–16x, and "clock speed" remarks (8, 9) are not about stability.
3. The f × T law (11 Nov night): required stability scales with carrier × window; the spec is mission-critical only at sub-THz over minutes or without sync.
4. Sub-THz coherent measurement as the proof phase (3, 1, 2): pain confirmed, buyers pay for references, LOI offered.
5. Sync-denied coherent sensing as the deployment phase (3, 5, 4): coherence surviving denial of the sync link; L-band space-surveillance radar removed as a clock-limited account after the model fix.
6. Coherent wireless infrastructure as the platform (4, 5, 6): devices as clients, infrastructure nodes without a wire, trusted time with PQC.
7. Model bug found and fixed (12 Nov, 04:20): quartz comparisons softened 2–8x; the wedge was restated narrowly and every number corrected.
